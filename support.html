Getting started

How do I connect?

Open the app, go to the STATUS tab, and tap CONNECT. The first time, iOS asks permission to add a VPN configuration — approve it with Face ID or your passcode. The status then changes to ".UP". Tap DISCONNECT to turn it off.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up or login. You can install the app and use every part of it without creating an account.

The connection won't come up.

Check that you have a working internet connection, then try again. If iOS never showed the VPN permission prompt, delete any half-added VolaVPN entry under Settings › General › VPN & Device Management and reconnect. Toggling Airplane Mode off and on, or restarting the app, clears most transient failures.

Can I use my own server?

Yes. In the CONF tab you can enter your own server credentials instead of the built-in one. This is optional — the app works out of the box without it.

The network toolkit

What can the tools do?

The TOOLS tab runs seven on-device diagnostics: a latency probe, DNS resolver lookups, port reachability, a throughput test, a local-network scan, an egress IP / ASN lookup, and captive-portal detection. They run from your device against the network you are on.

Where do the tool results go?

Nowhere. Results are shown to you on the device and are never uploaded or stored on a server.

Why do throughput values sometimes show "—"?

A dash means the value has not been measured, rather than a made-up number. VolaVPN never shows figures it did not actually measure.

Privacy & billing

Do you keep logs of my traffic?

No. VolaVPN does not log the contents or destinations of the traffic you send through the tunnel. The full details are in the Privacy Policy.

How much does it cost?

VolaVPN is free to download and use. There is no subscription and no in-app purchase.

How do I delete my data?

Because the app does not collect personal data or create an account, there is nothing stored on our side to delete. Removing the app from your device removes its on-device data.
