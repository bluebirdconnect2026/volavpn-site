What it does
Connect

One-tap secure with a live status readout: link state, session time, throughput.

Nodes

Searchable server list with per-node round-trip time and packet loss — pick the fastest route, not a random flag.

Toolkit

Ping, DNS lookups, throughput test, port reachability, egress IP/ASN, local-network scan and captive-portal probe.

Monitor

Your live connection at a glance — Wi-Fi or cellular, online state, whether the VPN is protecting you, metered and low-data flags — read straight from the system.

Latency timeline

A latency history that builds while you use the app: average, p95, max and loss, measured on-device — not a single one-off ping.

Privacy-first

A tool, not a data funnel. Diagnostics run on your device; no account is needed to explore the app.

Straight answers

Does it keep logs of my traffic?

No. VolaVPN does not log the contents or destinations of the traffic you send through the tunnel. See the Privacy Policy for exactly what is and isn't handled.

Do the network tools send my data anywhere?

The diagnostics (ping, DNS, port and speed checks, etc.) run from your device against the hosts you test. Results stay on your device.

Do I need an account?

No account is required to open the app and use the interface.
