Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 15, 2026

VolaVPN is built as a tool, not a data funnel. This policy explains, in plain terms, what the app does and does not do with your information.

Summary What we collect Stored on your device VPN traffic Network tools Third parties Security Children Your choices Changes Contact
Summary
  • No account is required to use the app.
  • We do not log the contents or destinations of your VPN traffic.
  • Diagnostics (ping, DNS, traceroute, etc.) run on your device; their results stay on your device.
  • Session history and network security scores are stored locally on your device, not on our servers.
  • We do not sell your data, and we do not use third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.
Information we collect

VolaVPN is designed to work without collecting personal information. Specifically:

  • No account, no profile. You are not asked to register, and we do not ask for your name, email, or phone number to use the app.
  • No advertising identifiers. The app does not gather advertising IDs and contains no third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.
  • Support contact (only if you write to us). If you email support, we receive the address you write from and whatever you choose to include, and use it solely to respond to you.
Information stored on your device

To make the app useful, some information is kept locally on your device and is not transmitted to us:

  • Your selected server, favorites and recent choices.
  • Session history (start/end time, server, duration, throughput counters).
  • Per-network security scores and flags (e.g., OPEN, WEAK-CRYPTO, CAPTIVE, DNS-HIJACK) for Wi-Fi and cellular networks you have joined.
  • Your settings and automation rules.

You can remove this data at any time by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app.

VPN traffic

When you connect, VolaVPN establishes an Encrypted tunnel and routes your device's traffic through the selected server. We do not log the websites you visit, the contents of your traffic, or DNS queries associated with your browsing. A VPN server necessarily processes packets in order to forward them; VolaVPN's design goal is to keep no record of that activity tied to you.

If you use servers that are not operated by us, that operator's own handling of traffic is governed by their policies, not this one. Where VolaVPN provides its own servers, the no-traffic-logging commitment above applies.
Network diagnostic tools

The built-in tools (latency probe, DNS lookup, traceroute, throughput test, DNS-leak check, port reachability, MTU discovery, egress IP/ASN, local-network scan, captive-portal probe) run from your device against the hosts you choose to test. Their results are shown to you and stored locally; they are not uploaded to us. Some tools (for example, egress IP lookup) necessarily contact an external endpoint to answer the question you asked — that request originates from your device.

Third parties

VolaVPN does not include third-party advertising or analytics SDKs and does not sell or rent your information. Apple processes your App Store purchase and delivery under Apple's own privacy policy; we do not receive your payment details.

Security

VPN sessions use a modern encrypted protocol. Data kept on your device is stored within the app's sandbox provided by the operating system. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, but we aim to minimize what is collected in the first place.

Children

VolaVPN is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age required by your local law.

Your choices
  • Use the app without an account.
  • Clear locally-stored history, scores and settings from within the app, or by uninstalling.
  • Contact us with any question or request about your information.
Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date above. Material changes will be reflected on this page.
